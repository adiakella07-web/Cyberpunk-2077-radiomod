Neon Dial
Requires: Cyber Engine Tweaks, RED4ext, RadioExt.
Open Apple Music (or Spotify) on your PC and load a playlist. Launch the game; tune to "Neon Dial FM"
(last station) and it plays. Tune away and it pauses. Control panel: http://127.0.0.1:8765
NeonDial.dll starts NeonDial.exe with the game and stops it when the game closes.
Log: %USERPROFILE%\.neondial\companion.log
