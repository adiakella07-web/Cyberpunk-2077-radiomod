# Neon Dial: Your Music as a Radio Station (Nexus page draft)

**Short description:** Adds "Neon Dial FM" to the radio list and plays your Apple Music / Spotify through the game's radio mix, with its own icon, song titles on the radio screen, distance fall-off for radio boxes and an optional radio texture. No developer accounts or API keys.

## Modes
- **Music app:** uses Apple Music / Spotify already open on your PC (play/pause via Windows media controls, audio captured into the game).
- **Standalone Spotify:** no app needed. Appears in Spotify as the device "Neon Dial FM" (Premium).
- **Standalone Apple Music:** no app needed. Runs the Apple Music website in a built-in window; sign in once.

## Requirements
Cyber Engine Tweaks, RED4ext, RadioExt, Windows 10 (2004+) / 11.

## Installation
Install with Vortex, enable, deploy. Launch the game, open the CET overlay and use the "Neon Dial" window.

## Privacy
Nothing is uploaded; logins are never stored by Neon Dial itself, account details are scrubbed from logs and the panel, and there is a one-click sign-out. Details: SECURITY.md in the repository.

## Known issues / limits
- Unsigned helper programs may trigger antivirus false positives; source and build scripts are on GitHub.
- Muffling behind walls is not possible with what RadioExt exposes.
