## 2. Things only you can do (and my honest flags)
1. **AI disclosure (required, and it must be accurate).** The code, build scripts, documentation and the icon-drawing script were written by an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) at your direction; you tested it in the game and reported what to fix. Use Nexus's AI tag that matches that reality, i.e. **AI-generated content**. **Do not choose "AI-assisted"** (that is for human-led work) and do not describe the code as hand-written. Nexus's rules and what they allow can change; re-read the AI policy page before you publish.
2. **"Original work" and Donation Points.** The Donation Points rules ask that files are your original work, or assets cleared for commercial use, or used with permission. Third-party parts here are open-source (licences included), and the AI-generated code's authorship is a grey area I can't judge. Nexus decides eligibility; if you are unsure, ask Nexus support before relying on it.
3. **RadioExt (and CET/RED4ext).** Nexus says content mainly built to work with someone else's content needs those authors' approval. Neon Dial adds a station for RadioExt and its CET addon reads RadioExt's internal data (to fix radio-box volume and show song titles). Check RadioExt's permissions on its Nexus page and **ask its author** (template below). List all three as required mods.
4. **Internet-connecting executables.** Nexus restricts programs that connect to the internet unless crucial to the mod. Send the staff note below when you submit, with the source link.
5. **CD PROJEKT RED's fan content guidelines.** They restrict commercial use of their IP and have taken down a mod behind a paywall (news, January 2026). Neon Dial is free and open source with no paywall, and Donation Points are a Nexus reward, but read CDPR's guidelines and decide for yourself; I can't give legal advice.
6. **Third-party terms.** librespot is an unofficial Spotify client and the Apple window automates Apple's website; either could conflict with those services' terms. That is why the Spotify part is optional and the page says so. Choose what you are comfortable publishing.
7. **Antivirus.** Unsigned programs sometimes get false positives. The folder build is less likely to than a single packed exe, but check the Nexus virus scan result and say so on the page if it flags.
8. **Donation Points opt-in** is done in your Nexus account (mod rewards area) after your page is published; follow the current instructions in the rules page above.
9. **Screenshots** must be your own (the CET overlay window, the radio screen with a song title, the icon in the station list).

## 3. Mod page: ready to paste
**Name:** Neon Dial - Your Music as a Radio Station
**Category:** Audio (or Miscellaneous/Utilities, whichever Nexus offers for Cyberpunk 2077)
**Version:** match the release tag (e.g. 1.6.0)
**Short description:** Adds "Neon Dial FM" to the radio list and plays your Apple Music or Spotify through the game's radio mix, with its own icon, song titles on the radio screen, radio-box distance fall-off and an optional radio texture.

**AI disclosure (put this near the top of the description):**
> AI-generated content: the code, build scripts and documentation of this mod were written by an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) under my direction. I tested it in-game and directed the fixes. The icon is drawn by a script (no image-generation model, no third-party art).

**Description:**
> Neon Dial adds a station, "Neon Dial FM", as the last entry of the in-game radio list. Tune to it with the normal radio controls and your own music plays; tune away and it pauses and keeps its place.
>
> **Three ways to use it** (switch in the "Neon Dial" window of the CET overlay):
> - **Music app:** use Apple Music or Spotify already open on your PC.
> - **Standalone Apple Music:** no app needed; a small built-in window shows the Apple Music website, you sign in once.
> - **Standalone Spotify (optional add-on file):** no app needed; it shows up in Spotify as the device "Neon Dial FM" (Spotify Premium).
>
> **Extras:** custom station icon, current song on the radio select screen, radio boxes respect the Radioport volume setting and fade with distance (and are quieter inside a car), optional sound texture (radio / lo-fi / warm tape), and a "sign out and erase saved logins" button.
>
> **Privacy:** Neon Dial never stores your Apple/Spotify password, has no telemetry, uploads nothing, and scrubs account details from its logs. Details: SECURITY.md in the source repository.
>
> **Logins and internet:** the main file does not connect to the internet by itself. Standalone Apple Music loads Apple's own website in a built-in window (you sign in there). The optional Spotify add-on connects to Spotify, which is how it plays your music.
>
> **Please note / known limits:** tested by the author on one Windows PC so far. When Windows only provides silence for an app's audio, Neon Dial detects it and plays through your speakers instead of the game's radio mix (Standalone Spotify is the most reliable way into the radio mix). Muffling behind walls isn't possible. The programs are unsigned and may trigger antivirus warnings; the full source is public.

**Requirements (list as required mods):** Cyber Engine Tweaks, RED4ext, RadioExt. Windows 10 (2004+) / 11. Standalone Apple Music also needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (already on Windows 11 and most Windows 10).

**Files to upload:** `NeonDial-<version>.zip` as the **Main file**; `NeonDial-SpotifyAddon-<version>.zip` as an **Optional file** named "Spotify add-on (Standalone Spotify mode)".

**Permissions (your choice; suggestions that match the MIT licence):** others may use, modify and redistribute with credit; Donation Points permission for assets: only if you are comfortable with it.

**Changelog (first release):** "First public release: three playback modes, custom icon, song titles, radio-box physics, texture, sign-out."

## 4. Message to Nexus support (internet-connecting executables)
> Hello, I'm submitting "Neon Dial" for Cyberpunk 2077. It contains small helper programs (a companion app and an audio helper).
